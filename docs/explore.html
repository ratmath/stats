A distribution calculator that prints only digits it can certify, exact summaries of data you type, and an experiment: choose how dirty the data are and see which estimator copes.

A distribution

Parameters are exact: type 1/3 or 0.05. Raise the digits and the precision follows.

Your data

Numbers separated by commas or spaces. Fractions such as 7/2 are welcome.

Two columns: compare, or fit a line

As two samples, the columns get Welch's t test and the F test for equal variances (and the paired t test, when they have the same length). As pairs, they get the least squares line with exact coefficients, the slope's 95% interval and the correlation test. See tests for means and linear regression.

An experiment

The truth is 10 and the clean noise is normal with standard deviation 1. A share of the observations, set by the slider, get noise with the larger standard deviation instead. The table shows each estimator's root mean squared error, and how often the usual ttt interval contains the truth.
